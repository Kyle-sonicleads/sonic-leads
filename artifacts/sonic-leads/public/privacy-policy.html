Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This privacy policy explains how North East House Clearance collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when requesting a quote through our Facebook lead form.

Who we are

North East House Clearance is a lead generation service connecting people in the North East of England with local waste removal and house clearance providers.

Contact: kyle@sonicleads.co.uk

What data we collect

When you submit a quote request, we collect the following information:

  • First name and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Postcode

How we use your data

We use your information solely to arrange a free quote for house clearance or waste removal services. Your details will be passed to a local service provider in your area who will contact you directly to discuss your requirements and provide a quote.

We do not use your data for any other marketing purposes without your consent.

Who we share your data with

Your details will be shared with one local waste removal and house clearance service provider operating in the North East of England, for the sole purpose of providing you with a quote. We do not sell your data to any third parties.

How long we keep your data

We retain your information for up to 12 months from the date of submission, after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to the data we hold about you, request that we correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at kyle@sonicleads.co.uk.

Cookies

This privacy policy applies to data collected via Facebook lead forms only. We do not operate a standalone website and do not use cookies to collect data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at kyle@sonicleads.co.uk.
